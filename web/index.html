How LifePlan AI Works

CA3 Multi-Agent Architecture

1. Hierarchical Orchestration over a Shared Blackboard

Unlike rigid sequential pipelines, LifePlan AI is a true autonomous multi-agent system powered by LangGraph. The Adaptive Coordinator acts as the supervisor, inspecting the blackboard state after every turn to decide the next best action: delegating to a specialist, initiating a parallel critique round, or finalizing.

2. The Five Specialist Agents

🤖 Adaptive Coordinator

Main decision-maker. Manages supervisor routing, evaluates conflict severities, enforces guardrails, and drives adaptive re-planning.

🧠 Personal Profile & Routine

Parses calendar histories (.ics / RRULE) to infer recurring routines. Computes true free windows with commute buffers and raises clashes.

📍 Discovery & Grounding

Grounds places in real-world APIs (Geoapify, OpenStreetMap Overpass). Verifies live events with Tavily/SerpApi and checks Open-Meteo weather.

🚗 Travel & Logistics

Calculates multi-modal travel times (OSRM road network, transit, walking), flight/hotel estimates, currency conversions, and budget limits.

📅 Planner & Optimization

Schedule solver that optimizes times, routes, opening hours, and pacing. Interacts peer-to-peer with Logistics and Discovery when needed.

3. Parallel Review Rounds & Conflict Resolution

Once a draft itinerary is built, Profile, Discovery, and Logistics review the schedule in parallel fan-out (LangGraph Send). If an opening hour clashes with arrival time, or travel exceeds budget, a conflict is detected. The Coordinator identifies the conflicting constraints and routes it back for an intelligent revision.

4. Real-World Data Grounding

All venue locations, road network travel durations, weather conditions, and foreign exchange rates are grounded in live services: Geoapify, Open-Meteo, OpenStreetMap, Tavily, SerpApi, and Frankfurter.
