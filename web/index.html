How LifePlan AI Works

Sequential, Consumer-Friendly Workflow

LifePlan AI guides you through 4 simple steps: describing what you want to do, picking your preferences, checking your existing commitments, and creating your itinerary. You don't have to fill in every field—sensible defaults and explicit instructions work together seamlessly.

Natural-Language Date & Time Precedence

If you mention dates or times directly in your request (for example, "Tomorrow from 3 PM to 6 PM"), our parser prioritizes your exact words over form pickers. The system will never silently drift your activities to another time window.

Autonomous Multi-Agent Collaboration

Under the hood, five specialized AI agents coordinate through a shared blackboard using LangGraph:

  • Coordinator: Orchestrates tasks, identifies constraints, and manages revisions.
  • Profile & Routine: Protects your free time and schedule commitments.
  • Discovery: Finds verified venues and live events with weather safety.
  • Logistics: Calculates real road-network travel times and checks budgets.
  • Planner: Solves and optimizes the final day-by-day sequence.

🔗 Connect Google / Apple Calendar

Connect your calendar once, and LifePlan AI will automatically fetch your live appointments and classes on its own whenever you create a plan.

How to get your private iCal link:

  • Google Calendar: On your computer, open Google Calendar → Click ⚙️ Settings → Click your calendar on the left → Scroll to Secret address in iCal format → Copy the URL.
  • Apple iCloud Calendar: Open iCloud / Mac Calendar → Click the share icon next to your calendar → Turn on Public Calendar or copy the webcal:// subscription link.
  • Local file: Alternatively, drop any calendar.ics file right into your project folder. LifePlan AI detects it automatically!
