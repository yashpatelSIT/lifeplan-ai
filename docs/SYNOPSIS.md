# LifePlan AI — Project Synopsis

**Problem.** People often have free time but find it hard to use well. Planning a few free hours in their own city, a full day, a weekend, a vacation or a business trip means weighing many things together: where they are, travel time, budget, preferences, work or college hours, existing appointments, weather, opening hours and what is actually on. Existing apps each cover one piece (a calendar, a map, a travel site, a recommendation feed). None of them combine all these constraints, so the user ends up doing the coordination by hand and plans often break, for example when an event clashes with a meeting or a restaurant turns out to be closed.

**Solution.** LifePlan AI is a multi-agent AI system in which five LLM agents, each with its own tools and responsibilities, plan together. The **Personal Profile & Availability** agent learns the user's weekly routine from calendar history, or takes manually entered hours, and works out when the user is really free, commute included. The **Discovery & Location** agent finds real venues and live events near the chosen location and checks the weather. The **Travel & Logistics** agent measures real travel times, picks transport modes and handles inter-city travel, flights, hotels and currency. The **Planner & Optimization** agent turns all of this into a schedule that balances time, distance, cost, preferences, opening hours and activity duration. The **Adaptive Coordinator** leads the team. It decides which agent works next, runs critique rounds where the agents check the draft from their own angle, resolves conflicts (an event at 19:00, a meeting until 19:30 and a 40-minute ride means the plan must change), and re-plans when the weather, a delay, a cancellation or the user's schedule changes. Every venue in a plan comes from a map database or a live search result with a source link. This grounding is our answer to the hallucination risk raised in the project review. The system is built with LangGraph, can use a different LLM for each agent with automatic fallback, has a web UI that streams the agents' conversation live, and records a full execution trace of every run.

**Target users.** Students and young professionals who want to make better use of their free time around college or work ("what can I do right now?", "plan my evening after the meeting", "plan my weekend"). Travellers planning domestic or international trips. Business travellers who need to fit meetings, travel and evenings together. The same five-agent architecture covers all of these, from a 2-hour local plan to a multi-day international trip, without adding new agents.

**Scope relative to the approved proposal.** The scope matches the approved CA2 description: the same five agents, conflict resolution and dynamic re-planning. We refined three points. (1) The technology stack is now explicit: LangGraph + LangChain, Gemini/Groq LLMs and FastAPI, which addresses the reviewer's comment. (2) We added explicit **grounding**: venues only come from Geoapify/OpenStreetMap, events must carry a live source URL, and a validator rejects anything not in the verified pool. (3) Train and bus options are estimated from distance, because there is no free official rail API. Flight and hotel prices are live when a SerpApi key is configured and are otherwise clearly labelled as estimates. "Learning" of routines and preferences uses statistical routine detection over calendar history plus feedback-weighted preferences, rather than a trained ML model.
